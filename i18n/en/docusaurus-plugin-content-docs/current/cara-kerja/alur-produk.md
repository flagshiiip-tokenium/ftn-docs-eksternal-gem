## Step by step

1. **Get the app.** Install Flagshiiip from the official [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flagshiiip), then sign in or create an account. See [Install the App](../panduan-pengguna/install-aplikasi.md).
2. **Scan the QR.** Point the app's camera at the Flagshiiip sticker on the product packaging. See [Scan the QR](../panduan-pengguna/scan-qr.md).
3. **See the result.** The app shows a certificate of authenticity if the product is verified. See [Authenticity Verification](./verifikasi-keaslian.md).
4. **Claim a reward (if available).** Products in a brand loyalty program may show a **Claim Reward** option. See [Claim a Reward](../panduan-pengguna/klaim-token.md).
