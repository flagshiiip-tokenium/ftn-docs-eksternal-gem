# Product Ownership Record

After a product QR code is scanned successfully, the app displays a digital authenticity certificate. The app also supports recording product ownership for future reference.

## What you can do

- Look back at the certificate of authenticity for products you have scanned.
- Record product ownership for future reference.

## When is it useful?

- When you want to show the product's authenticity certificate.
- When you want to check again on a product you bought earlier.
