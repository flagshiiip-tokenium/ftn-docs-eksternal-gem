# What is Flagshiiip?

Ever wondered whether a product you bought is genuine or counterfeit? Flagshiiip helps you verify it in seconds.

Every product protected by Flagshiiip features a **special smart sticker with a unique QR code** on its packaging. Simply scan that code using the mobile app, and the app instantly confirms whether the item is authentic.

## What You Get

- **Instant Authenticity Verification**: Just scan and the result appears on screen in seconds.
- **Official Digital Certificate**: Cryptographic proof stored in your phone that your item is 100% genuine.
- **Digital Ownership Records**: Keep a verified digital registry of the products you own directly inside the app.
- **Loyalty Rewards**: On supported products, receive permanent FTN token rewards from brands upon verification.

## Two Pillars of the Ecosystem: flagshiiip.com & flagshiiip.io

Flagshiiip unifies real-world physical product security with the power of digital assets across two primary platforms:

1. **[flagshiiip.com](https://flagshiiip.com)** — **Brand Solutions & Physical Product Protection**  
   Focuses on anti-counterfeiting technology, smart QR stickers, supply chain traceability, and comprehensive telemetry dashboards for brand owners and manufacturers.
2. **[flagshiiip.io](https://flagshiiip.io)** — **Tokenium & Digital Loyalty Ecosystem (FTN)**  
   Embodying the vision *"Where Loyalty Becomes an Asset"*. Focuses on transforming shopping loyalty into real digital assets (FTN Token), Polygon blockchain smart contracts, and gasless certificate minting for consumers.

## Who Uses Flagshiiip?

- **Brand Owners & Manufacturers**: Affix Flagshiiip smart stickers to product packaging to eliminate counterfeiting risks and build lasting, high-value customer loyalty.
- **Buyers & Consumers**: Scan packaging stickers with phone cameras to guarantee genuine goods, secure digital ownership certificates, and claim FTN token rewards.

You do not need to understand the underlying technical complexity to use Flagshiiip. All verification and claiming workflows take place automatically behind the scenes.
