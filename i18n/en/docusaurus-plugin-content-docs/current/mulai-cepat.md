# Quick Start

Flagshiiip helps you make sure the product you bought is truly authentic. It is easy and all you need is your phone.

## Three steps

1. **Get the app.** Install **Flagshiiip** from the official [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flagshiiip), then sign in or create an account.
2. **Scan the QR.** Point the app's camera at the Flagshiiip sticker on the product packaging.
3. **See the result.** If the product is authentic, a certificate of authenticity appears on your screen.

Products included in a brand loyalty program may show a **Claim Reward** option after scanning.
