# Scan the QR

The scanning view displays **Scan QR** and prompts you to place the QR code near the camera.

<figure className="docScreenshot">
  <img src="/scanqr.png" alt="Flagshiiip app Scan QR view with the camera frame and scanning instructions" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The QR scanning view in the Flagshiiip app.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Steps

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Open the QR scanning feature.
3. If asked, allow the app to use the camera.
4. Point the camera at the QR code on the Flagshiiip sticker on the packaging.
5. Place the QR code inside the scanning frame and wait for the certificate or verification result.

How to read the result is in [Authenticity Verification](../cara-kerja/verifikasi-keaslian.md).

## Tips for easy scanning

- Scan in a well-lit place.
- Hold your phone steady.
- Make sure the sticker is not dirty, damaged, or covered.
