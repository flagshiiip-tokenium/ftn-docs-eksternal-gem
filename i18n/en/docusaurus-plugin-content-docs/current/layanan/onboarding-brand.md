# Brand Onboarding

Joining the Flagshiiip ecosystem to protect your physical products is designed to be quick, secure, and seamless without disrupting your existing manufacturing operations.

## 4-Step Onboarding Process

1. **Consultation & Plan Selection**  
   Choose the service package that fits your business scale ([Starter or Custom](./paket-layanan.md)). Visit [flagshiiip.com](https://flagshiiip.com) to consult with a Flagshiiip enterprise representative.
2. **Product Registration & Unique Code Generation**  
   Register your product batch details (product names, variants, and unit quantities). The Flagshiiip system generates encrypted, cryptographically unique codes (*nonces*) ready for smart sticker printing.
3. **Smart Sticker Printing & Application**  
   Smart stickers are manufactured using durable, tamper-evident materials. Stickers are affixed to outer retail packaging or factory safety seals.
4. **Dashboard Activation & Live Scanning**  
   Your brand monitoring dashboard is officially activated. As products reach retail distribution and are scanned by consumers, telemetry data and geolocation maps update in real time.

## What Brands Should Prepare

- Brand name, product variants, and official distribution permits (if applicable).
- Estimated sticker volume requirements per production batch.
- High-resolution brand logo files and visual identity guidelines (for the Custom plan).
- FTN token reward program preferences (if activating customer loyalty features).
