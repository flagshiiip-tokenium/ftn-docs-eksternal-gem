# Blockchain, Explained Simply

You **do not need to understand this page** to use Flagshiiip. It is for readers who are curious about the technology powering the platform behind the scenes.

## Imagine a Shared Notebook

Imagine a notebook with copies held by many people simultaneously. Every time a new entry is made, all copies are updated together. If someone secretly tampers with a note in their own copy, everyone else's copies will expose the discrepancy, immediately flagging the alteration.

That is the foundational concept of a **blockchain**: a decentralized digital ledger stored in multiple nodes at once, making unauthorized modifications virtually impossible.

## What Does This Have to Do with Flagshiiip?

Flagshiiip leverages blockchain technology to power the following capabilities:

- **Immutable Product Authenticity**: Your scan results are backed by permanent, tamper-resistant records.
- **Digital Certificates of Authenticity**: Cryptographic proof of genuine ownership accessible within the mobile app.
- **Verified Ownership Histories**.
- **Permanent Loyalty Rewards** delivered as FTN tokens.

## Why Did Flagshiiip Choose the Polygon Network?

Flagshiiip operates on the **Polygon (Ethereum Layer-2)** blockchain, chosen for key enterprise and consumer advantages:

- **High Speed (< 2 Seconds)**: Instant validation and claiming without latency.
- **Eco-Friendly**: Utilizes a highly energy-efficient Proof-of-Stake consensus architecture.
- **Zero Gas Fees for Consumers (*Gasless*)**: Powered by relayer infrastructure so buyers never pay network or transaction fees when verifying or claiming tokens.
- **Audited Enterprise Security**: Smart contracts adhere to **OpenZeppelin** industry standards and have been verified and audited by premier Web3 cybersecurity firm **CertiK**.
