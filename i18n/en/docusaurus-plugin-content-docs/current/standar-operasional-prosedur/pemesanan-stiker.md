# Sticker Ordering

This guide explains the step-by-step procedure for ordering stickers through the Flagshiiip Ecosystem Dashboard, from account registration to monitoring sticker distribution. It applies to both new and registered business users.

## Account Registration and Verification

If you already have an account, proceed by signing in through the [Dashboard login page](https://dashboard.flagshiiip.com/login).

1. Open the [registration page](https://dashboard.flagshiiip.com/register), or click **Sign Up** on the login page.
2. Enter your company or business name, active email address, password, and confirm password.
3. Open the verification email from Flagshiiip. Check your Spam folder if it is not found in your inbox.
4. Click **Verify Email Address** in the email and ensure the success confirmation appears.
5. Click **Log In**, then sign in with your registered email and password.

## Completing Company Profile

The company profile must be completed and verified before ordering features become available.

1. Click **Complete Profile** on the main dashboard, or go to the **Profile** menu and select **Edit Profile**.
2. Enter company name and address, company category, Tax Identification Number (NPWP), PIC (*Person in Charge*) name, and PIC contact details.
3. Upload required documents:
   - **Company logo:** maximum 1 MB with a 1:1 (square) aspect ratio.
   - **Company and PIC documents:** maximum 2 MB per file. Ensure file names correspond accurately to their contents.
4. Read the **Terms and Conditions**, then check the confirmation box.
5. Click **Save Changes** and await verification by the Flagshiiip team before proceeding to register products.

## Registering a Product or Brand

1. Open the **Products** menu, then click **Add Product**.
2. Fill in the product name and product category.
3. Enter your official store URL in the **Webcommerce** field (e.g., Shopee, Tokopedia, etc.).
4. In **Contact Order**, enter the recipient's WhatsApp link using the format `https://wa.me/` followed by the phone number.
5. Add your official product or company website URL if available, then write a brief product description.
6. Upload a product logo (maximum 1 MB, 1:1 square aspect ratio) and required product documentation. Ensure file names reflect document contents.
7. Review the entered data, then click **Save Product**.

## Placing a Sticker Order

1. Open the **Orders** menu, then click **Create Order**.
2. Select the product for which stickers are to be ordered.
3. Fill in **Additional Notes** if there are extra instructions or specifications (can be text, images, or links).
4. Choose your desired **Sticker Type** and **Print Option**: **Physical** or **Digital (Non-physical)**.
5. Enter the quantity. The minimum order is **1,040 stickers**; orders must be in multiples of 1,040.
6. Review order details, then click **Proceed to Order** to navigate to the payment page.
7. Select a payment method and complete the transaction according to the instructions provided.
8. Upon payment completion, wait for confirmation from the Flagshiiip team regarding shipment of stickers to your destination address.

## Monitoring Sticker Distribution

- Open the **Home** menu to view a high-level summary of the data.
- Open the **Analytics** menu to track detailed distribution metrics and product performance.

## Troubleshooting

If you experience technical or administrative issues during registration, profile verification, product registration, or ordering, contact the Flagshiiip Team via the WhatsApp button located at the bottom-right corner of the dashboard.
